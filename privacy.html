Riwaaj Agent — Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Riwaaj Agent ("the app") is a private, internal bookkeeping tool built for and used only by Riwaaj Event Decor ("the business"). It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks users.

What the app accesses

With the business owner's authorization, the app connects to the business's own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 service. It reads and writes accounting records for that one company (such as invoices, estimates, customers, items, expenses and deposits) and reads basic identity information (name and email) of the person who authorizes the connection.

How the data is used

Data is used only to keep the business's own books: preparing invoices for the owner to review, categorizing transactions, and producing reports for the owner and the business's accountant. The app never sets or changes prices and never sends invoices on its own.

Storage and security

The app runs on a single computer controlled by the business. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never published. QuickBooks data remains in QuickBooks Online.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Data may be reviewed by the business's own accountant or tax preparer at the owner's direction.

Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in QuickBooks Online or in the Intuit account. Once disconnected, the app can no longer access the company.

Contact

Riwaaj Event Decor — the business owner.
